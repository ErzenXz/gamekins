# Gamekins community guidelines and safety controls

Gamekins is an independent library launcher. Friends' names, presence, activity text and other player content come from connected Epic Games, Steam or GOG accounts. Store pages and games are operated by their providers. Gamekins does not pre-screen provider content or operate those providers' moderation systems.

The Microsoft Store edition does not display, read or send native Gamekins chat. Use the provider's own client or game for conversations. Steam chat can open in Steam; GOG profiles can open in GOG. Epic's chat and reporting features vary by game, so Gamekins links to Epic's official safety help instead of offering an unsupported chat shortcut.

## Expected conduct

Do not use Gamekins or connected social features to share illegal content, child sexual exploitation or abuse material, threats of violence, targeted harassment, hateful abuse, scams, malicious links, or someone's private information without consent. Respect the provider's community rules and other players' boundaries. These guidelines do not replace the rules of the game or service you are using.

## Hide, block or remove locally

Open Friends, select the person's **More** menu, and choose **Hide in Gamekins**, **Block in Gamekins**, or **Remove from Gamekins list**. Incoming friend requests also offer **Block locally**.

All three controls suppress that account's friend entry, requests, unread badges and messages in the Gamekins Friends panel. They are saved in this device's local Gamekins profile and remain after restarting the app. They apply across connected accounts in that local profile. They do not change a provider friendship, prevent the provider from receiving communications, stop communication in another application, or block someone on the provider's network.

Use **Local safety controls → Restore** in Friends to undo a restriction. Clearing the app's profile data removes local restrictions. If saving fails, Gamekins shows an error rather than claiming that a restriction was saved. **Remove provider friendship…**, when available, is a separate action that asks the provider to remove that friendship.

## Report a concern privately to Gamekins

Choose **Report to Gamekins…** beside a friend, or **Report a safety concern…** in Friends. This opens your mail application addressed to [erzen@quad-labs.com](mailto:erzen@quad-labs.com?subject=Gamekins%20safety%20report), with the subject **Gamekins safety report**. You review, edit and send the message yourself. Gamekins never automatically sends a report or uploads conversation history.

For a friend-specific report, the draft includes the provider account ID and display name. Explain what happened, when, and where. Include supporting evidence only if you choose; avoid passwords, sign-in codes and access tokens. Reports sent to this address are private correspondence with the Gamekins maintainer, not public posts. The maintainer can investigate Gamekins behavior and help route provider content concerns; only the provider can take action against an account on its network. There is no guaranteed response time or provider enforcement outcome.

## Provider reporting and blocking

- **Epic Games:** use the relevant game's reporting tools for player conduct. For network blocking, open the player's profile from the Epic Games Launcher's Friends list and choose Block. See [Epic reporting instructions](https://safety.epicgames.com/policies/reporting-misconduct) and [Epic Launcher blocking instructions](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-32735058/c-44802862/epic-games-launcher-a19131266).
- **Steam:** open the person's Steam profile and its options menu to report the player or block communication. See [Steam's instructions for abuse, harassment and misconduct](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/4F8E-AE41-B6B1-F836).
- **GOG:** use the profile or conversation controls available in GOG for blocking. For misconduct or content reports, [contact GOG Support](https://support.gog.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) and identify the account and relevant content. GOG provides its own [content reporting policy](https://support.gog.com/hc/en-us/articles/16033687971613-Infringing-Content-and-Copyright-Policy-effective-from-17-February-2024).

The Friends panel's **Report or block with provider…** action opens the corresponding official help page. A local Gamekins block does not submit a provider report. Availability and labels in provider clients may change.
