# Gamekins — brand brief

**Gamekins** is a friendly desktop game launcher: one cozy home for all your games (starting with your
Epic Games library). It must feel **kid-friendly and welcoming for families**, while still looking
polished and premium for adult gamers. Think the warmth and instant likability of Duolingo's owl or
Nintendo characters, but with a completely **original** design.

## The mascot: a "Gamekin"
- A small, round, huggable creature: soft rounded body, big friendly shiny eyes, a happy little
  smile, tiny stubby arms/feet, and two small rounded ears or nubby antennae.
- **Signature detail:** a little golden **star** on its forehead or belly (a nod to the app's earlier
  star logo, and to "your favourite games").
- Body colour: bright friendly **sky blue → electric blue** (#5FD1FF → #1A9FFF) with a light
  belly/face patch (#F1F7FF). Accent: warm **sunny yellow/orange** (#FFC83D → #FF9F1C) for the star
  and small highlights. Optional soft pink cheeks.
- Style: clean modern vector illustration, soft gradients and gentle shading, crisp outlines are
  optional; consistent across all deliverables (same character, same proportions, same colours).
- Must read clearly at 16–32 px (simple silhouette, big eyes) and look great at 1024 px.
- Must NOT resemble existing characters or logos: no Kirby, Pokémon, Pac-Man, Sonic, Mario,
  Duolingo owl, Discord's Wumpus, Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation or Xbox. No guns, no scary
  elements. No text inside the icon.
