# Lodestar — brand brief

**Lodestar** is a fast, beautiful desktop game launcher. It replaces clunky store launchers
(starting with Epic Games) with one polished, Steam-quality home for your whole library.
Name meaning: a lodestar is the guiding star sailors navigate by. Tagline: *Every game. One home.*

## Visual direction
- Mood: premium, calm, modern, a little cosmic. Think "night sky navigation" meets gaming.
- Core motif: a bright four-point guiding star (like a north star / compass rose sparkle),
  optionally with a subtle orbit arc or light trail suggesting motion and launching.
- Palette: deep night navy (#0E141B → #171D25), electric blue (#1A9FFF), cyan glow (#5FD1FF),
  touch of warm starlight white (#F1F7FF). No purple-heavy gradients, no neon rainbow.
- Shape language: geometric, crisp, symmetric, works at 16px and at 1024px.
- Style: flat-to-soft-gradient vector look, subtle inner glow. NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D clay,
  NOT cartoon. No text inside the icon itself.
- Must NOT resemble the Steam, Epic Games, Valve, PlayStation or Xbox logos.

## Deliverables (PNG, transparent background unless noted)
1. `icon-1024.png` — app icon: rounded-square (squircle) tile, night-navy background with the star mark centered. 1024×1024.
2. `mark-1024.png` — the star mark alone on a transparent background (for in-app use), 1024×1024.
3. `wordmark.png` — horizontal lockup: star mark + the word "Lodestar" in a clean geometric sans, white text, transparent background, wide aspect (~3:1).
4. `banner.png` — README hero banner 1600×600: dark night-sky backdrop with a faint star field, the lockup on the left, and room on the right; tasteful, minimal.
